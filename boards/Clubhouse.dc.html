WEEK 6 RECAP✦ Written by Fanball AIPosted Tue 9:00 AM

The Syndicate drops 161 and the rest of the league is chasing

Commissioner's note Trade deadline is Sat, Nov 8. If you're 1-5, now's the time to sell. You know who you are.

Top dog, again. The Gridiron Syndicate put up the week's best score for the second time this season, 161.1, behind 31.4 from Jahmyr Gibbs and 28.6 from Jalen Hurts. The Puka Principle never had a chance, finishing at 108.5.

Closest call. Henry's Holes edged Chubb Club 118.2 to 117.6 when Kareem Hunt punched in a 1-yard touchdown with 1:12 left. Chubb Club left 24.0 points on its bench, including a 19-point day from Joe Mixon.

Blowout of the week. Certified Lamb Daddy beat Goff Ball 148.7 to 79.0. Malik Nabers had 27.2 before halftime.

Playoff race. With eight weeks left, the Syndicate and Lamb Daddy sit at 5-1. Four teams are tied at 3-3 for the last three spots, and Purdy Good Team (1-5) is two games out.
