How waivers run

  1. Round by round. Each team's highest open claim is in play: group 1 first, its top player first.
  2. Highest bid wins the player. Equal bids go to the team higher in waiver priority.
  3. A win closes the group. Its drop happens, the bid comes off your FAAB, and the rest of that group is skipped.
  4. A loss moves you down the list to that group's next player, in the next round.
  5. Skipped, not lost: a claim you can no longer afford, or whose drop was already used, is skipped and shown in your results.
